# Stakeholder Impact Analysis

## 1. Victim

### Financial Impact

The victim may suffer direct monetary loss from fraudulent
transactions.

### Emotional Impact

Phishing and financial fraud can cause stress, fear and loss
of confidence in online services.

### Privacy Impact

Stolen credentials may expose personal or organizational
information.

---

## 2. Organization

### Financial Impact

The organization may incur investigation, recovery and
incident-response costs.

### Reputational Impact

Customers and employees may lose trust in the organization
if attackers successfully impersonate its services.

### Operational Impact

Compromised accounts may disrupt normal business operations.

---

## 3. Financial Institutions

Banks and payment providers may need to investigate suspicious
transactions, freeze accounts where appropriate and cooperate
with law-enforcement investigations.

---

## 4. Society

Successful phishing campaigns reduce public trust in digital
services and online payments.

Cybersecurity awareness and better authentication practices can
help reduce the impact of such attacks.

---

## Overall Impact

Operation Hydra demonstrates that cybercrime can create
financial, technical, emotional and reputational consequences
across multiple stakeholders.
