# Ethical Analysis – Operation Hydra

## 1. Privacy

Investigators may encounter personal information while examining
emails, devices, accounts and transaction records.

Only information relevant to the investigation should be collected
and handled securely.

## 2. Cryptocurrency Tracing

Investigators may attempt to trace cryptocurrency transactions.

However, blockchain addresses do not automatically identify the
real-world person behind them. Investigators must avoid assuming
identity without supporting evidence.

## 3. Honeypots

Honeypots can be used to study attacker behavior.

However, investigators must ensure that the activity does not
create unnecessary harm, entrap innocent users or violate legal
requirements.

## 4. Evidence Integrity

Digital evidence should be preserved carefully.

Investigators should maintain records showing:

- What evidence was collected
- When it was collected
- How it was handled
- What analysis was performed

## 5. Responsible Investigation

Investigators should avoid accessing systems that they do not
have permission to investigate.

The investigation should remain within authorized scope.

## Conclusion

Cybercrime investigation requires balancing effective
investigation with privacy, legality, evidence integrity and
protection of innocent users.
