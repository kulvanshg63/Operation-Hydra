# Operation Hydra – Case Description

## Case Overview

Operation Hydra is a simulated multi-stage cyber-crime campaign targeting employees of ApexTech Solutions Pvt. Ltd.

The attackers use social engineering and technical deception to obtain employee credentials.

## Attack Scenario

The simulated attack follows these stages:

1. The attacker sends a phishing email to an employee.
2. The email contains a link to a simulated spoofed login page.
3. The victim enters credentials into the fake page.
4. The attacker uses the stolen credentials for simulated unauthorized access.
5. A second phishing email delivers a simulated Trojan attachment.
6. The simulated malware records dummy activity.
7. The attacker attempts to obtain financial information.
8. A simulated fraudulent transaction is initiated.
9. The funds are transferred to a simulated mule account.
10. A portion of the funds is transferred to a simulated cryptocurrency wallet.

## Investigation Objective

The investigation aims to identify:

- The cyber crimes involved
- The phishing techniques used
- Evidence of spoofing
- Simulated malware behavior
- Indicators of Compromise
- The financial transaction flow
- Applicable legal frameworks
- Ethical considerations
- Potential impact on stakeholders

## Investigation Limitation

This project is completely simulated for academic purposes. No real credentials, financial accounts, malware, or victims are involved.
